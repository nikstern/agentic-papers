# Summary
This survey argues that communication is the core systems lens for understanding LLM-based multi-agent systems, and organizes the field by communication architecture, goals, protocols, strategies, paradigms, objects, and content.

# Why It Matters
It gives a sharper framework than generic agent surveys for analyzing how multi-agent systems actually coordinate, deliberate, and share state.

# Method / Setup
The paper proposes a two-level communication-centric framework spanning macro system-level design and micro interaction-level mechanisms, then surveys representative systems and identifies communication-specific challenges.

# Key Claims
- Communication is the central organizing principle of LLM multi-agent systems.
- System-level and interaction-level communication choices jointly determine coordination behavior.
- Future progress requires better protocols, efficiency, security, and multimodal communication support.

# Limitations
- The framework is analytic rather than a directly validated benchmark protocol.
- Because it is communication-centric, other systems dimensions receive less emphasis.
